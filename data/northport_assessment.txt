NORTHPORT CHEMICAL STORAGE FACILITY
2025 ENVIRONMENTAL SITE ASSESSMENT

Project ID: ENV-2025-067
Client: Northport Chemicals Pty Ltd

SITE SUMMARY

The facility operated as a chemical storage and distribution
warehouse between 1994 and 2021.

LABORATORY RESULTS

Groundwater sample MW-02:
- Trichloroethylene: 0.091 mg/L
- Benzene: 0.004 mg/L
- Lead: Not detected

Soil sample BH-08:
- Trichloroethylene: 3.2 mg/kg
- Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons: 640 mg/kg

ASSESSMENT FINDINGS

Elevated trichloroethylene concentrations were detected in
groundwater and soil.

Historical solvent storage activities were identified as the
likely source of contamination.
