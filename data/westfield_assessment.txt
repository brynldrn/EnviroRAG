WESTFIELD FUEL DEPOT
2024 ENVIRONMENTAL SITE ASSESSMENT

Project ID: ENV-2024-118
Client: Westfield Logistics Pty Ltd

SITE SUMMARY

The site operated as a fuel storage depot between 1978 and 2018.

LABORATORY RESULTS

Groundwater sample MW-11:
- Benzene: 0.045 mg/L
- Toluene: 0.120 mg/L
- Lead: 0.003 mg/L

Soil sample BH-02:
- Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons: 4,200 mg/kg
- Benzene: 8.4 mg/kg

ASSESSMENT FINDINGS

Petroleum hydrocarbon contamination was identified in soil
surrounding the former underground fuel storage tanks.

Benzene was detected in groundwater downgradient of the former
tank area.
