RIVERSIDE INDUSTRIAL SITE
2025 ENVIRONMENTAL SITE ASSESSMENT

Project ID: ENV-2025-041
Client: Riverside Manufacturing Pty Ltd
Assessment Date: 14 March 2025

SITE SUMMARY

The Riverside Industrial Site operated as a metal fabrication
facility between 1987 and 2019.

Soil and groundwater samples were collected from twelve monitoring
locations across the property.

LABORATORY RESULTS

Groundwater sample MW-03:
- Lead: 0.084 mg/L
- Benzene: 0.012 mg/L
- Arsenic: 0.006 mg/L

Groundwater sample MW-07:
- Lead: 0.031 mg/L
- Benzene: Not detected
- Arsenic: 0.004 mg/L

Soil sample BH-04:
- Lead: 420 mg/kg
- Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons: 1,850 mg/kg
- Arsenic: 14 mg/kg

ASSESSMENT FINDINGS

Elevated lead concentrations were identified in groundwater at
monitoring location MW-03.

Elevated lead and petroleum hydrocarbon concentrations were identified
in soil at BH-04.

The investigation concluded that historical metal fabrication
activities were the likely source of the lead contamination.

Further groundwater delineation around MW-03 was recommended.
